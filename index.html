What kind of work do you take?

Anything that involves writing code or handling data. Small automation scripts, data cleaning, database fixes, pipeline builds, reports, simple web tools. If it's outside my range, I'll say so on the first call.

How long does it take?

Small scripts: a few days. Full ETL pipelines: 1–3 weeks. Database fixes and reports: usually a week. Realistic timelines, in writing.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I work with clients across time zones. Communication via email, Slack, or whatever tool you already use.

How do you charge?

Fixed price per project, agreed upfront. Smaller jobs can be hourly if you prefer. I always give you the number before I start.

Who owns the code?

You do. Everything I deliver is yours — code, docs, and configuration. No lock-in, no hidden dependencies.

What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's normal — and that's what the first call is for. I'll help you figure out the scope before we commit to anything.
